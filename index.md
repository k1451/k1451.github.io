## 🔧 Setting Up Your First Bot

1. In the left panel, click "Add Bot" or the "+" icon.
2. Give your bot a name (like "Helper" or "Research Assistant").
3. Choose a model from the dropdown. If you don't have one configured, deskfolk uses a default that works out of the box.
4. Click "Create" and your bot appears in the chat list.
5. Type a message to your bot and hit Enter. It will respond using the model you picked.

To change models for a single message, look for a small selector near the input box. deskfolk will show a short note under the response explaining why that model was chosen.

- **Create a Task:** Type a task name and click "Add."
- **Move a Task:** Drag cards between columns like "To Do," "In Progress," and "Done."
- **Assign a Bot:** Click on a task and select a bot to handle it.

This is perfect for keeping track of small projects or daily to-dos without leaving your chat window.

## 🛠️ Getting More Advanced (Optional)

If you're curious about connecting your own AI models, here's the short version:

- **Endpoints:** In Settings, you can paste a URL to your own model server (like one from OpenAI, Anthropic, or a local setup). Just follow the format shown.

You don't *need* to do any of this to use deskfolk—it works fine on its own.
